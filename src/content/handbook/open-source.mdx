# Code, content and licences

Most of what volunteers make is public: code, data, articles, slides, this handbook.

## Open-source projects

- Every public repository has a **licence file**. Read it before you contribute. By opening a pull request you offer your contribution under that licence.
- Don't add code, data or images you are not allowed to share. Credit and keep the licence of anything you reuse.
- Don't commit secrets: passwords, API keys, tokens, private data. If you do by mistake, tell the repository's lead and the ED at once; deleting the commit is not enough, the secret has to be changed.
- Bringing an existing project of your own or your lab's? It stays yours. Agree in writing with the ED how NeuroTechX will host, fork or link to it before moving it into the organization.

## Content

Articles, talks and course material published by NeuroTechX carry the author's name. Agree the licence (often Creative Commons Attribution) with the project lead before publishing.

## What belongs to NeuroTechX

The name and logo, the website and social media accounts, the member, subscriber and Slack data, internal documents and records, and accounts and mailboxes. These stay with NeuroTechX when people change roles.
