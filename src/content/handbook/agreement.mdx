# The contributor agreement

Everyone who works on NeuroTechX itself signs the **Contributor Acceptance Form** once. It was written for us by our lawyers. This page explains it in plain language; the signed form is what counts.

You do not need it to take part in the community: public Slack channels, events and pull requests to public repositories are open to everyone under the Code of Conduct.

## What it says

**Your conduct (section 2).** You agree to:

- follow the [Code of Conduct](https://neurotechx.org/code-of-conduct) and the policies we share with you;
- treat everyone professionally; harassment of any kind ends your role;
- **not commit NeuroTechX to anything, or give opinions in its name, without approval** (see [Working together](/handbook/working-together));
- **respect people's privacy**: use their information only for your task, never make lists, export or share it (see [Personal information](/handbook/privacy));
- follow reasonable instructions, and tell us if you disagree so we can sort it out;
- obey the law, act in good faith, and not bring NeuroTechX into disrepute;
- **tell us about conflicts of interest** (below);
- not work for NeuroTechX while intoxicated;
- **keep your own brand or company separate** from NeuroTechX: you can talk about your venture, but not use the NeuroTechX name to promote it; keep NeuroTechX material off personal or company servers; use NeuroTechX accounts, not personal or company ones, for NeuroTechX business; and don't pay for NeuroTechX services with your own or your company's money.

**Intellectual property (section 3).** What you owned before stays yours. Unless we agree otherwise in writing, NeuroTechX owns what you produce for it. Suggestions about how NeuroTechX runs become its property, and you waive moral rights in them.

**Confidential information (section 4).** Things we share with you that are not public, such as finances, plans, partner discussions and personal data, are for your NeuroTechX work only. When you leave, or when we ask, you return or delete them, including any personal information.

## Conflicts of interest

A conflict is anything that could make it look as if you are acting for yourself, an employer or a company rather than for NeuroTechX. Examples: you work for, advise or own part of a neurotechnology company; you are negotiating with a sponsor who is also your client; your lab would benefit from a decision you are helping make.

Having one is normal and rarely a problem. **Disclose it in writing to the Executive Director** ([mhough@neurotechx.com](mailto:mhough@neurotechx.com)): once when you sign, and again whenever something new comes up. The ED records it (a conflict-of-interest register is being set up) and tells you if you should step back from a particular decision. When in doubt, disclose.

## How you sign

The person onboarding you sends the form. Sign and return it. The signed copy is filed in a restricted folder, and the date and version are recorded in the agreement register. Only the Executive Director and the board can see them.

## Paid work is different

The form is for unpaid volunteers. Anyone paid by NeuroTechX for a piece of work signs a separate contractor agreement first.
