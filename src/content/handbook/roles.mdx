# Roles

Volunteer roles are described in the same short format, so newcomers know what they are taking on and leads know what they are offering. Open roles are advertised on [Join the Team](https://neurotechx.org/join-the-team).

## Role card

Copy this for a new role:

- **Role**:
- **Project and lead**:
- **What you do**: three to five concrete tasks
- **Time**: hours a month, and for how long (for example "4 hours a month for six months")
- **You'll need**: skills, or "none, we'll show you"
- **Access**: Slack channels, GitHub team, folders, email address or not
- **First task**: something finishable in two weeks
- **You'll get**: what the volunteer learns or gains

## Roles we are looking for

As advertised on Join the Team (November 2026):

- **Student clubs liaison**: answers studentclubs@, welcomes new clubs, keeps the club list current, introduces club officers to each other.
- **Content Lab writers, editors and designers**.
- **Chapter organizers**: start or restart a city chapter; one public event a quarter.
- **Website and open-source contributors**: the website and projects such as EEG-ExPy and MOABB.
