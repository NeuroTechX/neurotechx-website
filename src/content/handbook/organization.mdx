# How NeuroTechX is organized

## The board

Société BCI Montréal is governed by a **board of directors**, elected under the by-laws. The board approves the budget, policies, agreements above the Executive Director's authority, and the election of officers. Board decisions are minuted.

## The Executive Director

The **Executive Director (ED)**, currently Morgan Hough, who is also President, runs the organization day to day: volunteers, partners, accounts, the website and newsletter. Unless a page says otherwise, "ask the ED" means write to [mhough@neurotechx.com](mailto:mhough@neurotechx.com).

## Officers

The **Treasurer** looks after the bank account, payments, the books and reimbursements. The **Secretary** keeps minutes, registers and the compliance calendar. Ask the ED who holds each role today.

## Projects and leads

Most work happens in **projects**: the website, the newsletter, the Content Lab, a hackathon, an open-source repository, a chapter or club programme. Each project has a **lead**, who is a volunteer. The lead decides how the project works, onboards its volunteers with the ED, and is your first point of contact.

A project without a lead is paused, not abandoned. If you want to lead one, say so.

## Role mailboxes

Shared addresses belong to roles, not people. When a role changes hands, the mailbox does too.

Each .org address delivers to the same mailbox as its older .com twin. Use .org in anything new.

## NeuroTechX and NTX Services

NTX Services is a **separate company**. It is not part of NeuroTechX, and the partnership between them has ended (see [the public statement](https://neurotechx.org/ntx-services-partnership-termination)). Do not use NeuroTechX accounts, lists or materials for it, and do not present it as NeuroTechX.
