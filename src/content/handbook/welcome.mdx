# Welcome

Thank you for giving NeuroTechX some of your time. This page is the short version of everything else in the handbook.

## Who we are

NeuroTechX is an international community for people interested in neurotechnology: students, researchers, engineers, clinicians, artists and founders. We run a website and newsletter, a Slack workspace, local chapters and university clubs, the Content Lab, hackathons, and open-source projects.

Legally, NeuroTechX is the operating name of **Société BCI Montréal**, a non-profit corporation registered in Quebec in 2015 (NEQ 1170984166). It is a non-profit, **not a registered charity**: we can give business receipts but not charitable tax receipts. Contracts, grants and bank matters always use the legal name.

NeuroTechX has no paid staff. It is run by a volunteer board, an Executive Director and volunteers like you.

## What we ask of you

- **Do what you said you would, or say early that you can't.** Both are fine; silence is not.
- **Follow the** [Code of Conduct](https://neurotechx.org/code-of-conduct) **everywhere you act for NeuroTechX**, online and in person.
- **Protect people's information.** Member lists, emails and sign-up data are never copied out, exported or reused. See [Personal information](/handbook/privacy).
- **Keep NeuroTechX separate from your own projects and employer.** See [The contributor agreement](/handbook/agreement).
- **Ask before you speak for NeuroTechX** to the press, a sponsor or a partner.

## What you get

*Draft: proposed, not yet adopted.*

- A real project with an owner who answers you, and a first task in your first week.
- Access to the community: Slack, events, the people in our network.
- A letter or reference describing your work, on request, once you have contributed.
- Credit: your name on the work you did, where the work carries names.

## The first two weeks

1.  You write to us. We answer within three working days.
2.  A short call with the person who will look after you. You agree a first task.
3.  You sign the contributor agreement (online, a few minutes).
4.  The same day you get Slack and access to what your task needs.
5.  A check-in after 30 days.

The details are in [Joining](/handbook/joining).
