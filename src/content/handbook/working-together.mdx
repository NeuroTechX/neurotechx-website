# Working together

## Where we talk

- **Slack** for day-to-day work: ask in your project's channel rather than in direct messages, so others can help and the answer stays findable.
- **Email** for anything to people outside NeuroTechX, and for decisions that need a record.
- **Video calls** on Google Meet. Share notes from any call where something was decided.
- Most of us volunteer around jobs and studies, across time zones. Expect replies within a few days, not minutes. If something is urgent, say so in the first line.

## Getting decisions made

- Your **project lead** decides how the project's work is done.
- The **Executive Director** decides anything that commits NeuroTechX: agreements, money, partnerships, public statements, new accounts or services, and merges to the website.
- The **board** decides budgets, policies and anything above the ED's authority.

When you need a decision, write it as a question with a recommendation: "Can we do X? I suggest yes, because Y." You will get an answer faster.

## Speaking for NeuroTechX

The contributor agreement asks you not to commit NeuroTechX to anything or give opinions in its name without approval. In practice:

- You can describe NeuroTechX and your project, point to the website, and invite people to events.
- **Ask the ED first** before you agree a partnership, sponsorship, price or date with an outside organization; talk to journalists; endorse a product or company; post on the official social media accounts; or sign anything.
- On your own accounts, say "I volunteer with NeuroTechX", not "NeuroTechX thinks".

## Using the name and logo

The NeuroTechX name and logo identify the organization's own activities. Use them for NeuroTechX projects and events. Don't use them for your company, course, product or another organization's event without the ED's written approval.

## Credit

Say who did the work. Put contributors' names on articles, talks and release notes where the work carries names. Don't present someone else's work, or AI-generated text, as your own original work.

## Code of Conduct

The [Code of Conduct](https://neurotechx.org/code-of-conduct) applies in Slack, in meetings, at events and in every space we run. Report a problem to **admins@neurotechx.org**. If the report concerns someone who reads that address, write directly to another director listed on [neurotechx.org/about](https://neurotechx.org/about). Reports are kept confidential.
