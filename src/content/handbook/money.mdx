# Money

NeuroTechX runs on a small budget, mostly from sponsorships and donations. Every dollar goes through the organization's own bank account and books.

## Spending

- **Ask before you spend.** Any cost, however small, is approved by the Executive Director before you commit to it, by email or in writing in Slack.
- **Don't pay NeuroTechX bills yourself** and don't put NeuroTechX services on your own card or account. If a payment is approved and there is no other way, it is reimbursed (below).
- **No new subscriptions or paid accounts** without the ED's approval, even on a free trial. Bills go to [billing@neurotechx.org](mailto:billing@neurotechx.org).

## Reimbursement

> **Draft.** A written reimbursement procedure is not adopted yet. Until it is:
>
> 1.  Get approval before spending.
> 2.  Send the receipt and a one-line description to [billing@neurotechx.org](mailto:billing@neurotechx.org) within 30 days.
> 3.  The Treasurer reimburses by bank transfer.

## Sponsorship and donations

- **Write to the ED before you agree anything with a sponsor**: amount, what they get, logos, dates.
- Sponsors pay NeuroTechX directly and receive an invoice and a receipt. We are a non-profit, **not a registered charity**: we cannot issue charitable tax receipts, and you should never suggest that we can.
- Sponsorship money raised for an event or chapter is used for it: paying vendors or reimbursing organizers, with receipts.
- Donations go through [neurotechx.org/donate](https://neurotechx.org/donate).
