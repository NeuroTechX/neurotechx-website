# Stepping back or leaving

People's lives change; stepping back is normal and you are always welcome back.

## If you are leaving

1.  **Tell your project lead and the ED**, ideally two weeks ahead.
2.  **Hand over**: write down what is in progress, where it is, and who should take it.
3.  **Transfer ownership** of any document, folder, calendar or account you created for NeuroTechX to your lead or the ED. Files you own in your personal Google account disappear when you delete them.
4.  **Return or delete** NeuroTechX confidential information and personal data you hold, on your computer, phone or personal accounts, and confirm you have done so.
5.  You can keep your Slack membership as a community member.

## What we do

Within a week of your last day, your lead or the ED:

- removes you from private Slack channels and GitHub teams and repositories;
- unshares project folders and transfers your files;
- suspends your NeuroTechX email account, or turns it into a forward or hands it to your successor if it was a role address;
- removes you from shared logins and changes any password you knew;
- updates the volunteer tracker, and the website if you were listed;
- thanks you, and offers a reference letter.

## If someone goes quiet

After three months without contact, the lead writes once. With no answer after another two weeks, access is removed as above, and the door stays open.
